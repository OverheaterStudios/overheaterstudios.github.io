About

A small studio that runs hot

Overheater Studios is an independent studio making games and tools. Small team, big ideas, too much coffee.

Who we are

We're players first. Overheater started because we kept wanting games and tools that didn't exist yet, so we started building them.

The team is small on purpose. The people who make each game or tool are the same people who patch it, answer bug reports and read the feedback, so when you write in, you're talking to someone who knows the code.

We've built across games, VR and XR, mobile and the web, and we bring all of it to what we make next.
